How to Play Linear Arrangement

Each question has a series of linear arrangement relationships. For example:

  • Chicken is adjacent and right of Cinema
  • Bike is two steps right of Chicken

To solve the puzzle, arrange the subjects linearly based on the premises.

A conclusion states the same kind of relation — "Cinema is adjacent and left of Bike". True if your arrangement agrees, False if it does not. Distances are exact: adjacent is not two steps.

The Inversion Cue

Whenever you encounter a phrase highlighted in red, it's an indication that you need to think inversely about the information presented.
